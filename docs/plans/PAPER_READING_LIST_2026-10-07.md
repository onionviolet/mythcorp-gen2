# Reading list for the cybercrime paper, 2026-10-07

Paywalled papers with no open copy I could find, for the next pass on
`/wc/papers/ai-cybercrime`. Download them through the W&L library and keep the
PDFs outside the repo. Every citation below was checked against its Crossref
record on 2026-10-07. Priority is how much the paper would change if the
article said something unexpected.

Already used from open copies, so not listed: Collier et al. 2021 (BJC
accepted manuscript), Collier et al. 2019 (IMC author copy), Li and Paxson
2017, Allodi 2017 (arXiv), and the arXiv preprints.
